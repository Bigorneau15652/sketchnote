## 0.40.0

English

Floating menus for the forms. A button that has several forms (the bubble, the festoon, the point and the eraser) turns active when it is chosen, shows the form that is chosen as its own pictogram, and opens a floating menu on its left with the other forms from top to bottom (four bubbles, eleven festoons, twenty-two shapes of the point on two columns, three tips of the eraser). A click on a form chooses it; the bar at the top of the floating menu moves it, and it stays where it was put. The menu stays open while the tool is active; a second click on the button closes it and a third opens it again. The choices of the forms are no longer in the menu of the bottom, which keeps the sizes and the colours. In the bottom row, the bar of the undo and of the zoom is now on the left and the menu on the right.

Français

Des menus flottants pour les formes. Un bouton qui a plusieurs formes (la bulle, le feston, le point et la gomme) change de couleur quand il est choisi, montre la forme choisie comme pictogramme et ouvre à sa gauche un menu flottant avec les autres formes de haut en bas (quatre bulles, onze festons, vingt-deux formes du point sur deux colonnes, trois pointes de gomme). Un clic sur une forme la choisit ; la barre du haut du menu flottant le déplace, et il reste où on l'a mis. Le menu reste ouvert tant que l'outil est actif ; un deuxième clic sur le bouton le ferme et un troisième le rouvre. Les choix de formes ne sont plus dans le menu du bas, qui garde les tailles et les couleurs. Dans la rangée du bas, la barre d'annulation et de zoom est maintenant à gauche et le menu à droite.

## 0.39.0

English

A lighter look. The buttons of the column on the right are as small as those of the ribbon of Obsidian (30 pixels), and the bottom bar is thinner. The menu is now one thin line, in the manner of the bar of Affinity, and it has no text any more: every choice is a pictogram or a small field, and its name is its tooltip. The sliders of the menu are replaced by fields: a pictogram, the value with its unit, a small arrow that opens a list of usual values, and a double click on the value to type it (Enter keeps it, Escape leaves it; the value is kept inside its range). The zoom of the bottom bar is the same: a minus button, a field with the level in percent (list of usual levels from 25 to 400, or a typed value) and a plus button; the zoom slider is gone. The colours are in one compartment: the round swatch of the border, the one of the fill and the three types of line (continuous, dashes, dots), with one thin slider of transparency under the swatches. For a shape, a click on the type of line that is on takes the border away (a click on another type puts it back), and the swatch of the border becomes a crossed circle; a line, an arrow or a pen stroke keep their line. The transparency of a shape is the transparency of its fill (the border stays sharp), and that of anything else is the transparency of the whole element; at 100 % the swatch of the fill becomes a crossed circle too (the least that a file allows stays drawn: 5 %). The shadow, the reflection, the vertical text, the crop and the conversion of an icon into strokes are buttons with a pictogram instead of boxes to check. The menu still scrolls sideways when the window is narrow. The setting One word under each pictogram of the menu is removed (a saved value is ignored).

Français

Un aspect plus léger. Les boutons de la colonne de droite sont aussi petits que ceux du ruban d'Obsidian (30 pixels), et la barre du bas est plus mince. Le menu est maintenant une seule ligne mince, à la manière de la barre d'Affinity, et il n'a plus aucun texte : chaque choix est un pictogramme ou un petit champ, et son nom est son infobulle. Les glissières du menu sont remplacées par des champs : un pictogramme, la valeur avec son unité, une petite flèche qui ouvre une liste de valeurs usuelles, et un double clic sur la valeur pour la taper (Entrée la garde, Échap la quitte ; la valeur reste dans sa plage). Le zoom de la barre du bas fait de même : un bouton moins, un champ avec le niveau en pourcentage (liste de niveaux usuels de 25 à 400, ou une valeur tapée) et un bouton plus ; la glissière du zoom a disparu. Les couleurs sont dans un seul compartiment : la pastille de la bordure, celle du remplissage et les trois types de trait (continu, tirets, points), avec une glissière fine de transparence sous les pastilles. Pour une forme, un clic sur le type de trait qui est actif retire la bordure (un clic sur un autre type la remet), et la pastille de la bordure devient un rond barré ; un trait, une flèche ou un tracé au stylo gardent leur trait. La transparence d'une forme est celle de son remplissage (la bordure reste nette), et celle de tout le reste est la transparence de l'élément entier ; à 100 % la pastille du remplissage devient aussi un rond barré (le minimum qu'un fichier permet reste dessiné : 5 %). L'ombre, le reflet, le texte vertical, le rognage et la transformation d'une icône en traits sont des boutons avec un pictogramme à la place des cases à cocher. Le menu défile toujours latéralement quand la fenêtre est étroite. Le réglage Un mot sous chaque pictogramme du menu est supprimé (une valeur enregistrée est ignorée).

## 0.38.0

English

The library of drawings. A selection of the page can be kept as a drawing and put back on a page (or on another one) later. To keep a drawing: select what must be kept (a zone is selected as one block with a click in the structure mode, or elements in the drawing mode), then use the new button Save in the library among the buttons of the selection (the menu): the window asks the name (a name with the date is proposed) and writes a drawing note (.sketchdraw) in the folder of the library, with a number after the name when it is already taken. The strokes of the structure of the page and the number of the zone are not kept; the groups and the sizes are. To put a drawing back: the new button Drawings of the column (after Pictures) opens the library as small views, with a search by name and the most recent first; a click on a drawing puts it in the middle of the view, at its size (smaller when it is wider than half of the view), grouped so that it moves as one block, selected, and in the zone that is being drawn. A new setting, Folder of the library of drawings (Sketchnote bibliothèque by default, created if it does not exist), says where the drawings are kept: they are ordinary notes that can be renamed, moved or deleted in Obsidian. The new button of the column is added next to the others in a saved order of buttons.

Un nouveau réglage, Dossier de la bibliothèque de dessins (Sketchnote bibliothèque par défaut, créé s'il n'existe pas), dit où les dessins sont rangés : ce sont des notes ordinaires que l'on peut renommer, déplacer ou supprimer dans Obsidian. Le nouveau bouton de la colonne est ajouté à côté des autres dans un ordre de boutons enregistré.

## 0.37.0

English

One page to structure and to draw. The page and the drawing are now the same place. In the structure mode the whole page is shown, with the dotted places of the structure and the menus of the page. A double click on a zone (an empty one, or one that has a drawing) enters the drawing mode of that zone: the view zooms on the zone, the column of tools and the menu of the drawing appear, and what is drawn is drawn directly on the page and belongs to the zone. The rest of the page stays visible, faded, with a slider for the fading (the menu Display, with the choice This zone alone or With the rest of the page); it cannot be touched. The whole page can be seen and moved over at any time: the button Show the whole page (and Back to the zone) of the bottom bar, the zoom and the hand work as everywhere. A drawing can go beyond the dotted outline of its zone. Escape (once the tool and the selection are ended), or the back button, returns to the structure mode, where the whole page is shown again. To draw in another zone, leave the zone and double click on the other one. A double click outside every zone enters the free part of the page (annotations, arrows between zones, a page that has no structure): what is drawn there belongs to no zone. In the structure mode a click on a drawing selects the whole zone as one block, which is moved, resized, turned, mirrored, locked or deleted as one (the actions on the selection are in the menu), and a zone follows its place when the structure or the size of the page changes (the drawing keeps its place in the zone, in proportion, and is never distorted). A copy of a zone is free: it does not join the zone of the original. The zones that have no drawing keep their dotted outline. The page file holds everything: each element has an optional field slot, the number of its zone. A page of an earlier version is converted when it is opened: the drawings that were kept in notes of their own are brought into the page at the place and the scale where they were shown, each one in its zone (the lines are scaled with them), the empty frames are dropped, and the old drawing notes stay in the vault as they were. Zen mode is now one window: it hides the menus of Obsidian (and can go full screen, a setting) on the page, which holds the whole page and the drawing at once; the floating window of the page is gone, and with it the settings of its place.

## 0.36.0

English

The Path structure is redone. Its arrow no longer winds down the middle of the page only: the Page window now has a Form of the arrow (Snake, Zigzag, Steps, Straight line) and a Direction of the arrow (from top to bottom, from bottom to top, from left to right, from right to left, and the four diagonals). The arrow crosses the page from one side to the opposite one. The steps are marked by a point drawn on the arrow, in order along it, and the zone of each step is beside its point, on alternate sides, beyond everything that the arrow can reach on that side, so that a zone never meets the arrow or another zone. The zones become smaller, or go to the other side of the arrow, when they would leave the page, and a drawing that is in a zone follows it when the form or the direction changes. The steps of the zigzag are its corners, and those of the staircase are the middle of its runs. The form and the direction are kept in the file (fields shape and direction of the structure); a page made before this version has the Snake from top to bottom, and the arrow and the zones are redrawn only when the page settings are changed. The number of steps is still from 3 to 10.

## 0.34.0

English

The eraser. A new tool in the column on the right (key E) that erases by passing over the drawing, like a rubber. It erases strokes, filled shapes, icons, pictures and texts where it goes, but nothing is destroyed: each pass is kept on the elements that it touched, written on the element itself (as a part of its box), and the element is drawn through a mask that the passes cut out. A pass therefore stays on the same part of the element when it is moved, resized, turned or mirrored, and a pass can be undone with Ctrl+Z. The button Remove the erasures, among the buttons that act on the selection, makes the selected elements whole again. The menu of the eraser chooses the tip (soft, with a blurred edge, round or square), the size and the density (from a light erasure to a complete one). The cursor shows the size and the form of the tip. Locked elements and the strokes of a page structure are not erased. The passes are checked when a file is read (at most 400 passes of 3000 points for an element). Elements drawn after a pass are not affected by it. A saved order of buttons gets the new button next to the Point tool.

Français

La gomme. Un nouvel outil dans la colonne de droite (touche E) qui efface en passant sur le dessin, comme une gomme. Elle efface les traits, les formes remplies, les icônes, les images et les textes là où elle passe, mais rien n'est détruit : chaque passage est gardé sur les éléments qu'il a touchés, écrit sur l'élément lui-même (en parties de sa boîte), et l'élément est dessiné à travers un masque que les passages découpent. Un passage reste donc sur la même partie de l'élément quand on le déplace, le redimensionne, le tourne ou le retourne, et il s'annule avec Ctrl+Z. Le bouton Retirer les effacements, parmi les boutons qui agissent sur la sélection, rend entiers les éléments sélectionnés. Le menu de la gomme choisit la pointe (douce, au bord flou, ronde ou carrée), la taille et la densité (d'un effacement léger à un effacement complet). Le curseur montre la taille et la forme de la pointe. Les éléments verrouillés et les traits d'une structure de page ne sont pas effacés. Les passages sont vérifiés à la lecture d'un fichier (au plus 400 passages de 3000 points pour un élément). Les éléments dessinés après un passage ne sont pas touchés par lui. Un ordre de boutons enregistré reçoit le nouveau bouton à côté de l'outil Point.

The chalkboard sets a white stroke. The page can be zoomed with the buttons of the bottom bar, with Ctrl or Cmd and the wheel or by pinching, and dragged with the mouse or one finger. Files of version 0.1.0 open without change.

Français

La page. Un bouton Page en bas de la vue ouvre les réglages de la page : format (A5, A4, A3, 16/9, carré ou dimensions libres en pixels), orientation, style de fond (blanc, lignes, quadrillage, points, papier craft ou tableau noir), couleur du fond et couleur du trait par défaut. Le tableau noir passe le trait en blanc. La page se zoome avec les boutons de la barre du bas, avec Ctrl ou Cmd et la molette ou en pinçant avec deux doigts, et se déplace à la souris ou avec un doigt. Les fichiers de la version 0.1.0 s'ouvrent sans changement.

## 0.1.0

English

First version. Creates a .sketchnote file (one JSON document holding the page and all its elements), opens it in its own view and shows an empty A4 page. The command and the ribbon button Create a sketchnote make a new file next to the active note. The file is read with strict checks (size, version, page, colours). Works on desktop and on mobile. Interface in English and French. Tests, build and release workflow are in place.

Français

Première version. Crée un fichier .sketchnote (un seul document JSON qui contient la page et tous ses éléments), l'ouvre dans sa propre vue et affiche une page A4 vide. La commande et le bouton du ruban Créer un sketchnote créent un nouveau fichier à côté de la note active. Le fichier est lu avec des contrôles stricts (taille, version, page, couleurs). Fonctionne sur ordinateur et sur mobile. Interface en français et en anglais. Les tests, la construction et le workflow de publication sont en place.
